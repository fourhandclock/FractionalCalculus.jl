1. Purpose and reference problem
This is a separate working document for the mathematical observations raised during a
study and numerical reproduction of the following article:
Y. A. Sabawi, S. Santra, P. Das, and N. A. Mohammad, H 1 stability and con-
vergence analysis of the L1/L1-2 Legendre spectral method for non-local weakly
singular integro-PDEs, Calcolo 62, article 42 (2025). doi:10.1007/s10092-
025-00671-y.
The main model is a time-fractional initial–boundary value problem with a weakly
singular Volterra memory term. In one space dimension, its structure is

The article uses L1 and L1-2 time discretizations, a combined trapezoidal/singular-panel
quadrature for the Volterra operator, and Legendre–Gauss–Lobatto spectral discretization
in space. Its analysis discusses time-step well-posedness, stability, and temporal and spatial
convergence.
The purpose of this document is not to declare the reported theorems false. It collects
individual mathematical questions, the evidence currently available, and the points that
remain to be independently derived or tested. An invalid step in a proof does not, by itself,
imply that the theorem is false. The original seven observations were initially provisional.
Observation 2 has a verified algebraic discrepancy (Section 5), while Observation 8 has a
verified quadrature counterexample and sharp consistency estimate (Section 4). Section 6
now independently audits the L1–2 portion of the stability argument and identifies a specific
failure of its coefficient-based induction; the theorem itself remains unresolved. The other
observations retain provisional status.

Observation 6 (Reported discrete error normalization). Status: numerical reproduction
check.
The numerical section defines discrete errors with spatial Lobatto weights and an
additional averaging factor involving (M + 1)−1 . The factor is not part of the standard
Lobatto quadrature approximation of an L2 norm, because the quadrature weights already
account for the spatial integration. During reproduction, report the article’s metric and the
conventional quadrature norm separately; otherwise apparent spatial rates may be affected
by the normalization.
Observation 7 (Kernel-sign assumption versus numerical examples). Status: assumptions
check.
The stability proof assumes a strictly positive spatial coefficient K(x, t). The paper’s
numerical Example 6.2 uses a factor K = x on a domain containing negative x, so that
hypothesis is not satisfied throughout the domain. Distinguish which results apply under
positivity and which experiments probe more general coefficients.

by the stated coefficient properties: the normalization d0 = 1 after startup is incorrect, and
signed history coefficients violate the required absolute-sum bound. The independent 2022
L1–2 analysis explicitly recognizes this sign difficulty and develops a different route to stability.

a corrected energy estimate with a suitable discrete memory/Gronwall argument, or a
parameter-valid counterexample, is still needed.
7. Verification protocol
Each observation will be considered individually. For each one, the verification record
should identify: (i) the exact statement and assumptions in the article; (ii) an independent
derivation or counterexample; (iii) a numerical experiment when relevant; and (iv) whether
the issue is a notation error, an unjustified proof step, a counterexample to a stated estimate,
or an unresolved question. Mathematical conclusions should be updated only when the
evidence is sufficient.

the full Volterra–diffusion recurrence using signed-coefficient bounds and then reassess the
