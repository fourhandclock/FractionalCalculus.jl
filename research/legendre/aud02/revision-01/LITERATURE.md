## Comparison with the nonnegative-matrix literature

Van Dijk–Sladký §4 compares matrix iterations and cumulative rewards using
monotonicity classes. Its equation (15) assumes preservation of a chosen
class by both matrices. Result 4.2 instead tests a difference of operators on
the specified reward trajectory. GR-04 constructs a Toeplitz majorant whose
own iterates remain ordered; it does not assume that the original matrices
preserve that cone. Thus the general iteration argument has prior art, but
the written hypotheses are not automatically identical.

The paper does not, in the inspected comparison statements, identify GR-04's
particular power-tail Toeplitz construction, arbitrary mixed words, shifted
simplex estimates and mesh-uniform binomial-resolvent limit as one theorem.
This is a limitation of the established comparison, **not evidence that the
combined result is new**. No proof of exact equivalence or of novelty was
obtained. The revised GR-04 manuscript cites this precedent and retains the
unresolved novelty qualification.
