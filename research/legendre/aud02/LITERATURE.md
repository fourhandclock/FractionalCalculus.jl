# Primary-literature and novelty review

The continuous positive-kernel resolvent and the multinomial Mittag–Leffler
representation are established machinery. Uniform and nonuniform Alikhanov
coefficient, complementary-kernel and consistency results also have direct
primary sources. The precise equivalence of GR-04's row-tail majorant theorem
to earlier discrete comparison results remains **unresolved**. The review
does not accept a novelty claim for it or for the familiar graded-mesh order
two threshold.

Primary-literature network access and downloads were explicitly authorized for
AUD-02. No dependency acquisition was authorized or performed. Retrieved PDFs
are local review copies in [literature/](literature/); URLs, versions, byte counts,
hashes and failed acquisitions are recorded in
[the acquisition inventory](evidence/literature-inventory.json). Downloaded
papers are not added to the original repository or prepared for publication.

The two LMZ preprints are different papers and have different hypothesis
numbering. GR-06's locators refer to **v4 of 1803.09873**, not the SIAM
discrete Grönwall paper. The ratio in v4 is old step divided by next step;
the bound is 7/4 in that orientation. Its Lemma 3.6 contains a maximum-based
consistency bound; the graded-rate calculation in the independent report
does not introduce an endpoint logarithm. Its constants may deteriorate with
the fractional order. The source does not itself certify our ordinary H1
Volterra/GLL extension.

## What the search does and does not establish

Queries combined the exact cited titles and terms such as `row-tail`,
`tail sums`, `positive kernels`, `discrete Gronwall`, `Toeplitz`, `Alikhanov`,
`Volterra memory`, `graded mesh` and `H1`. Technical conclusions above rely
on primary papers or publisher records, not search snippets, citation counts,
ResearchGate summaries or automated novelty claims. Bibliographic leads with
no accessible primary source are marked accordingly.

The row-tail integration-by-parts identity is elementary finite order
comparison. General positive-kernel resolvent bounds and weakly singular
discrete Grönwall theory precede this programme. GR-04's specific combination
of a power-tail class, commuting Toeplitz majorants, ordered words, an implicit
diagonal condition and a uniform binomial-resolvent limit may be a useful
presentation. Whether it is a distinct theorem or a specialization/reformulation
of existing work is unresolved. An unsuccessful exact-phrase search cannot
settle that question. No originality claim should be added to the manuscripts
on the strength of this audit.

Primary PDFs have searchable text sidecars for navigation. The Dixon–McKee
scan has imperfect OCR; the PDF is the source, and OCR is not treated as an
exact equation transcription. The initial host extraction emitted font warnings;
no new font/parser dependency was installed. Missing full-text comparisons
remain explicit limitations rather than fabricated source verification.
